Signs You Need Roofing Services

Identifying roofing problems early on can prevent costly repairs and protect your home from further damage. Here are some common signs that you may need roofing services:

  • Visible Leaks Inside Your Home: Water stains on ceilings or walls are a clear indication of a leaking roof. Addressing leaks promptly can prevent extensive water damage and mold growth. The key to preventing further water damage is to call us the moment you see a leak.
  • Missing or Damaged Shingles: Missing, cracked, or curled shingles expose your roof to the elements and can lead to leaks. Timely shingle replacement can prevent water damage and maintain the integrity of your roof.
  • Granules in Gutters: The presence of shingle granules in your gutters indicates that your shingles are deteriorating. This is a sign that your roof may be nearing the end of its lifespan and may need replacement.
  • Sagging Roof Deck: A sagging roof deck is a sign of structural damage and requires immediate attention. This can be caused by water damage, rot, or inadequate support.
  • Mold or Mildew Growth: Mold or mildew growth on your roof or in your attic indicates moisture problems and can damage your roofing materials. Addressing moisture issues promptly can prevent further damage and health hazards.
